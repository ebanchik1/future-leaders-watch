How We Screen

The "breadcrumbs before the market notices" approach behind this watchlist.
1
Stable base, 4–8 months. Look for names that have gone quiet — tight range, low volatility, no story — before anything moves.
2
Gradual breakout, not a spike. A slow grind higher over weeks beats a single headline-driven pop — spikes get chased and faded.
3
Rising volume. Steady, climbing participation confirms real accumulation rather than a one-day event.
4
Insider buying. Open-market cash purchases by officers/directors — not RSU vesting or routine grants — are the strongest single tell.
5
Institutional buying. New 13F/13G positions or meaningfully rising stakes from funds, ideally independent of news.
6
Real catalysts. Contract wins, FDA milestones, M&A chatter, or analyst upgrades — evidence the fundamentals are actually improving.
7
Small/mid-cap bias. Market cap generally under ~$10B, where a catalyst can still move the stock meaningfully.
